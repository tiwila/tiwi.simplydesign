## The Problem

TheraLink connects users with licensed therapists and provides tools for managing
well-being.

Users struggle to find therapists matching their values, face high costs, and privacy
concerns. TheraLink aims to make support accessible through an easy matching system
and strong data privacy.

Through interviews and surveys, I discovered users often feel overwhelmed by finding a
suitable therapist, especially when considering cost, compatibility, security concerns,
and emotional connection online.

## The Why Moment

The matching flow was the make-or-break moment. Users needed confidence before
committing to therapy, not just a searchable directory. I shifted the experience from
search-and-browse to guided decision-making centered on trust and fit.

## What I Did

### Designing an intuitive matching system
I designed a guided matching flow to reduce choice overload. Instead of an open directory,
users move through structured prompts that surface therapists based on compatibility.

### Making privacy visible, not buried
Privacy concerns showed up repeatedly in research, so I placed trust cues directly inside
the flow where hesitation occurred, rather than hiding them in settings.

### Adding personalised filter options
Users wanted stronger curation options. I introduced filters for therapist identity and
values to make selection feel personal instead of generic.

## What Broke

I initially assumed visual consistency would be enough to build confidence, but testing
showed users still hesitated because trust signals were not clear enough. I reworked the
flow to prioritize reassurance before commitment.

## The Outcome

TheraLink delivers a one-stop solution for mental health support through therapist matching
designed around values, not just price or availability. Usability testing with 5 participants
validated the direction: matching felt intuitive, privacy clarity improved, and users could
find therapists that felt right for them. The final system was also built with contrast,
keyboard navigation, and screen reader compatibility in mind.
